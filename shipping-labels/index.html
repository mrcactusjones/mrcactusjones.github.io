    Printer setup

    Edge gap keeps the label off the die cut. Shifts correct for your printer; use negative numbers to move left or up. Settings stay in this browser.

    How to print
    1. Open the PDF and print at Actual size or 100%. “Fit to page” shrinks everything and the labels will miss the cuts.
    2. Paper size Letter, one-sided. Print the alignment test on plain paper first, lay it on a label sheet, and hold both up to a window.
    3. Mark the top edge of a plain sheet before your first test so you learn which side and direction your tray prints.
    4. Reusing a partial sheet: feed it the same way as a fresh one. On a laser printer, use the manual or rear slot if you have one; gaps where labels were peeled can catch in the fuser.
    5. The label is scaled to fit 4 × 5. If the size reads under 80%, scan the barcode with your phone before you ship.
