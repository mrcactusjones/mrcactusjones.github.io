    How to print
    1. Open the PDF and print at Actual size or 100%. “Fit to page” shrinks the page and the addresses will drift off the labels.
    2. Paper size Letter, one-sided. Print the alignment test on plain paper first, lay it on a label sheet, and hold both up to a window.
    3. Cut each label along the middle, between the edge ticks. A paper trimmer gives the straightest cut.
    4. Reusing a partial sheet: feed it the same way as a fresh one. On a laser printer, use the manual or rear slot if you have one; gaps where labels were peeled can catch in the fuser.
