How it works
  1. Paste or open a list. You can also drop a .txt anywhere on this page.
  2. Swipe right on cards you want, left to pass, up for maybe. Maybes come back for a second look at the end.
  3. Hit Save wishlist to get a .txt of every line you swiped right on, copied exactly from your list and ready for deckbuy.
