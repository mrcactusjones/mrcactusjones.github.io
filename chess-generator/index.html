PRINTING IT

  1. Open the .3mf in Bambu Studio (File → Open Project). Printer, process and the two filaments are already chosen.
  2. Each board quarter and each army has its own plate. Slice with Slice all.
  3. On a quarter plate, map filament 1 (light) and filament 2 (dark) to your AMS slots when you send it.
  4. Army plates use one filament each: light, then dark.

THE BOARD

The board is the light filament. The dark squares are inlaid into its top layers, so they come out flush, and the filament only swaps a few times per plate.

Split boards join with dovetails on the square edges. Each tab is coloured like the square it sits in, so the pattern stays unbroken across the seams. Press the quarters together on a flat table. A drop of glue in each joint makes it permanent.

THE PIECES

Pieces scale uniformly so the king's base is a set fraction of a square (78% by default). The other pieces keep their proportions.

Print upright with tree supports where the shapes overhang. A1 mini users: the 180 mm bed also fits a 29.9 cm board's quarters, but has no room left for a prime tower.
